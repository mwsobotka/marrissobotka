April 2026
Senior capstone

Spatial Models, Electoral Systems, and Representational Outcomes

A computational spatial voting model comparing First-Past-the-Post, Approval Voting, and Instant Runoff in a two-dimensional electorate where parties adapt their positions over repeated elections. 1,000 Monte Carlo runs, validated with Condorcet-efficiency rates and paired t-tests.

Approval Voting produced the lowest voter-winner distance, highest welfare, and highest Condorcet-winner selection rate, p < 0.001.
